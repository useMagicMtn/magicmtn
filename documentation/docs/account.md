Open <AppLink href="/account">Account & security</AppLink> to manage your password, passkeys, and signed-in sessions.

## Sign-in methods

To create an account, open <AppLink href="/signup">Sign up</AppLink> and choose Google, GitHub, or email and password. GitHub signup uses your verified primary GitHub email.

You can sign in using a linked Google or GitHub account, your password, or an enrolled passkey.

To add a passkey, sign in and enroll it from your account page.

## Manage sessions

Review your sessions on the account page and revoke any you no longer use. Sign out from the app's account menu when finished on a shared computer.

For the CLI, run `magic login` to approve access in your browser. Run `magic logout` to revoke that CLI session.

## Preferences and billing

Open <AppLink href="/settings">Settings</AppLink> to manage preferences and custom instructions. Open <AppLink href="/billing">Billing</AppLink> to view the billing options for your account.
